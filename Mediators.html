Overview

What Is Mediation?

Mediation is a voluntary, private dispute-resolution process where an impartial third-party mediator helps disputing parties discuss interests openly and explore mutually acceptable written settlements—without handing decision-making control to an adversarial courtroom verdict.

Process

How Mediation Works

1. Submit Request

Share the dispute category, context, and preferred availability.

2. Review & Pairing

A neutral mediator matching the dispute domain and language is assigned.

3. Facilitated Dialogue

Parties engage in confidential joint or separate sessions to explore options.

4. Written Settlement

If both sides agree, settlement terms are recorded clearly for legal review.
